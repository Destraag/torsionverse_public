-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
GY-1 [NEEDS-CALL, deep-dive deferred by author, 2026-09-07] doc_gyroscope.txt's
  Abstract and Section 1 present Bernoulli medium drag as THE mechanism for
  gyroscope precession generally ("Gyroscope precession arises because...
  The restoring mechanism is BERNOULLI"), but Section 1's OWN numbers show
  this is negligible at lab/Earth scale: Bernoulli pressure reduction
  delta_P_medium ~ 1e-5 Pa vs air drag ~1500 Pa -- roughly 10^8 times too
  small to matter -- and its own text concludes "For lab gyroscopes: medium
  Bernoulli is negligible; standard mechanics applies." Ordinary classical
  mechanics (torque = dL/dt, precession rate omega_p = tau/L) already fully
  and correctly explains every real, testable gyroscope (tops, gyrocompasses,
  Earth's own axial precession) with no gap for a medium effect to fill.

  The document's own escape clause -- "At Zone 2 scale (molecular/nuclear):
  rho_eff ~ P_bond/v_s^2 >> rho_medium -> For atomic rotors, NMR, pulsars:
  medium Bernoulli IS the mechanism" -- is a different, much more exotic
  regime claim, and has NOT been separately verified here.

  RESOLVED ELSEWHERE (2026-09-07): the parallel claim in

  BERNOULLI") has been REMOVED (both the main bullet and an orphaned
