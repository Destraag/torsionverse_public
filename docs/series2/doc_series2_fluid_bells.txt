ABSTRACT
--------
A hand-held or vehicle-mounted thruster using two counter-rotating bell-shaped
shells of high-density liquid can, in principle, produce net thrust while
imparting zero reaction torque to the operator. The mechanism rests on I_h
character-table algebra: the symmetric spin mode (T_1g, angular momentum)
cancels exactly between counter-rotating units, while the asymmetric mode
(T_2g, generated by the curved bell wall breaking spherical symmetry) does
NOT cancel — because it depends on chi², which is insensitive to the sign
of Ω. This is not a new force; it is standard fluid dynamics (Taylor-Proudman
boundary layer torque, Coriolis over-roll, hydraulic wedge) amplified through
a torsionverse-derived T_2g/T_1g coupling ratio, plus a genuinely open
question of how efficiently that fluid pressure couples to the torsion
medium itself (η, not yet derived).

The choice of working fluid matters: torsionverse independently derives
(nuclear_geometry.py, not from any external claim) that Z=114/N=184 is a
doubly-magic nuclear shell closure, and predicts the once-synthesized
Mc-299 (Z=115, N=184) would couple to the T_2g thrust mode where mercury's
nuclear structure gives exactly zero coupling — a qualitative, not just
quantitative, difference.


SECTION 1: COUNTER-ROTATING DEVICE — MECHANISM
-------------------------------------------------
Reference: analysis/misc/new_ground.py III.1-III.5, Companion check AG1

Two co-axial bell-shaped or hemispherical shells, each containing a
high-density spinning fluid, counter-rotating (Shell 1 at +Ω, Shell 2 at −Ω,
both thrust axes aligned).

WHY T_1g CANCELS (no torque felt by the operator):
  Shell 1: T_1g winding, chirality +1 → angular momentum L = +IΩẑ
  Shell 2: T_1g winding, chirality −1 → angular momentum L = −IΩẑ
  Sum: ΔL = 0 → no net torque reaction on the device body / operator hand.
  chi(T_1g_1 + T_1g_2) at C5 = phi − phi = 0  (exact cancellation)  [AG1]

WHY T_2g ADDS (net thrust, does not cancel):
  Thrust scales as F ~ chi²(T_2g) × ρ_fluid × (ΩR)²
  chi²(T_2g) depends on |chi|², not the sign of chi — reversing spin direction
  does not change the sign of chi². For both units (same geometry, opposite spin):
    |chi(T_2g, C5)| = 1/phi  for BOTH shells
    Net T_2g force = 2 × chi²(T_2g) = 2/phi² = 0.7639  → DOUBLES  [AG1]

WHY THE CURVED PATH IS ESSENTIAL:
  A symmetric sphere spinning at ±Ω is pure T_1g — no T_2g component, zero
  thrust even if driven asymmetrically. A bell/paraboloid shape breaks
  spherical symmetry (O_h → C_4v or C_∞v via the curved wall's centrifugal-
  spiral path), which mixes in the T_2g component that survives reversal.

THE THREE FLUID-MECHANICS FORCE MULTIPLIERS (standard fluid dynamics,
amplifying the torsionverse T_2g coupling, from new_ground.py III.1):
  1. Boundary layer torque: T ~ ρΩ²R⁴f(Re)  — Taylor-Proudman scaling
  2. Coriolis over-roll: secondary flow amplifies the T_2g pressure gradient
  3. Hydraulic wedge: bell curvature reinforces the asymmetric pressure profile
  All three scale as Ω² → quadratic RPM curve is the falsifiable signature
  (new_ground.py III.2): a linear or cubic result would falsify the mechanism.

CONNECTION TO HISTORICAL DEVICES (noted, not claimed as evidence):
  Die Glocke descriptions: "two counter-rotating bell-shaped housings
  containing a rotating mercury-like fluid" match this geometry. This device
  configuration was derived independently from the torsionverse mechanics;
  the geometric convergence with historical descriptions is a coincidence
  worth noting, not supporting evidence — the prediction stands on the
  mechanics alone.

MEDIUM COUPLING EFFICIENCY η (GENUINELY OPEN):
  η = alpha × chi²(T_2g) × (coupling function of Zone 2 / device scale)
  This coupling function is NOT yet derived.
  AT LAB SCALE: medium coupling is negligible (medium Bernoulli << fluid
  Bernoulli by ~27 orders of magnitude, per gyroscope_doc.py GY2). The
  mechanism at lab scale is ordinary fluid dynamics amplified by the three
  multipliers above — not a medium-scale effect.
  AT HIGH-DENSITY / HIGH-RPM REGIMES: Zone 2 coupling may become relevant
  when the fluid's winding pressure approaches G_shear × γ_crit, which
  requires v_fluid approaching v_s = Rs×c = 5.33e7 m/s — not achievable
  with any known mechanical system.

ENGINEERING ESTIMATE (fluid dynamics only, no medium coupling assumed):
  Liquid mercury, R=5cm, 10,000 RPM: v_rim=52.4 m/s, ΔP_fluid=1.86e7 Pa
  F_wall per shell (chi²=1/phi²):        55.7 kN  (single bell structural load)
  F_wall both shells combined (chi²=2/phi²): 111.3 kN  (net T_2g, both bells)
  The factor of 2 comes from chi²(T_2g) being sign-independent: both shells
  contribute +1/phi² regardless of rotation direction.
  Force scales as Omega²  (quadratic RPM curve, not linear or cubic — AG2b).
  These are forces the fluid exerts on the containing structure — a
  structural engineering number, NOT thrust on external space. The thrust
  itself requires η (open above).

  High-density fluids (mercury, Mc-299) are needed on Earth to overcome

  RPM could let an astronaut set a travel vector without expending propellant.
  The chi² coupling and F ∝ Ω² scaling (AG2b) hold for any Newtonian fluid.

PRIOR ART
---------
  Closest existing EVA attitude control devices:
    SAFER (Simplified Aid For EVA Rescue, NASA, 1994–present):
      24 cold-gas nitrogen thrusters in a backpack. Expendable propellant;
      single-use emergency device. Produces thrust by expelling mass.
    MMU (Manned Maneuvering Unit, NASA, 1984, retired):
      Larger cold-gas unit, same expulsion principle.
    Reaction wheels / Control Moment Gyroscopes (CMGs):
      Used on spacecraft (ISS, Hubble) for attitude control. Solid rotating
      masses. Not used on suited astronauts. No fluid component.
    Fluid-ring gyroscopes:
      Exist for sensing (inertial navigation), not for propulsion.

  No publicly documented counter-rotating sealed-fluid bell device for
  astronaut attitude control or propulsion is known in the literature.

  What distinguishes the present mechanism (if η > 0 is confirmed):
    — No propellant consumed: fluid is sealed and recirculating
    — No exhaust plume: safe for proximity operations near crew/structures
    — Zero net torque on operator: T_1g cancels exactly (AG1), unlike a
       single-rotor gyroscope which torques the user's wrist
    — Throttleable by RPM: force ∝ Ω² (AG2b), continuous analog control
    — Buildable with off-the-shelf parts at the mechanical level regardless
       of whether the torsion-medium thrust (η) is confirmed

  The "Die Glocke" historical claim (counter-rotating drums of heavy liquid,
