THESIS
------

Three apparently unrelated phenomena -- piezoelectricity, acoustic amplification
at specific geometric apex angles, and apparent weight reduction in spinning
pentagonal structures -- are all manifestations of T_1g pressure-mode coupling
in the icosahedral elastic medium. The coupling efficiency in each case is
determined by chi(T_1g) from the I_h character table. The unifying quantity is
proximity to Maxwell-critical icosahedral geometry (3V-E=6).


ABSTRACT
---------

We derive a unified mechanism for piezoelectricity and geometric charge
concentration from the torsionverse I_h elastic medium framework. Applied
stress tunes a crystal toward the Maxwell-critical (3V-E=6) icosahedral
configuration, activating T_1g mode coupling and generating a voltage. The
same coupling, evaluated geometrically for a pentagonal pyramid with face
slope arctan(1/phi) = 31.72 deg, predicts maximum charge concentration at
the apex via a cell-compression channeling mechanism -- the "electron
escalator." The 5-sided pyramid achieves chi(T_1g, C5) = phi = 1.618; the
4-sided pyramid gives chi = -1 (defocusing); the 3-sided gives chi = 0
(null). The predicted apex charge ratio 5-sided / 4-sided = phi is
falsifiable at bench scale with an electrometer and three machined pyramids
for under $50. We identify retrodictions of existing acoustic and crystal
data and describe the discriminating experimental protocol.


SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION
------------------------

Three apparently unrelated phenomena -- piezoelectricity in quartz crystals,
acoustic amplification at specific geometric apex angles, and charge
concentration at pyramid tips -- are each explained by the same underlying
quantity: chi(T_1g) from the I_h icosahedral character table. In the
torsionverse framework the elastic medium has I_h symmetry, and the T_1g
irreducible representation carries the pressure/EM mode. Any physical geometry
that activates T_1g coupling will produce an observable electric or acoustic
response; the coupling strength is given exactly by chi(T_1g) evaluated at
the rotation angle that characterises the geometry.

This paper derives the mechanism for two cases: (1) piezoelectricity, where
applied stress drives a crystal toward the Maxwell-critical icosahedral
configuration and activates T_1g coupling; and (2) the electron escalator,
where a pentagonal pyramid with face slope arctan(1/phi) = 31.72 deg presents
exactly the icosahedral face orientation on all five faces simultaneously,
causing coherent cell compression along every face and channeling charge
directionally toward the apex. Both mechanisms are governed by the same
group-theoretic number, phi = chi(T_1g, C5) = 1.618, and both produce specific
falsifiable predictions at bench scale.

The dynamic extension -- apparent weight reduction in a spinning pentagonal
pyramid -- is covered in the companion paper doc_series2_spinning_pyramid.txt.


SECTION 2: MAXWELL CRITICALITY AND T_1g ACTIVATION
----------------------------------------------------

The icosahedral Jobson cell satisfies the Maxwell stability criterion 3V-E=6
exactly: it has 12 vertices and 30 edges, giving 3(12)-30 = 6. A truss
satisfying 3V-E=6 sits at the rigidity transition -- it is neither floppy
(underconstrained) nor over-rigid. At this critical point the medium supports
a soft mode: the T_1g pressure mode can propagate through it with zero
energy cost in the long-wavelength limit. Below criticality T_1g is gapped
(suppressed); above criticality the medium is rigid and T_1g cannot propagate
freely. Exactly at criticality: T_1g activates and any deformation that has
T_1g character produces an electric field response.

The coupling strength is chi(T_1g, C_N) where C_N is the rotation symmetry
of the deformation:

  chi(T_1g, C5)  = phi   = 1.618  [72 deg, icosahedral pentagonal axis]
  chi(T_1g, C4)  = -1           [90 deg]
  chi(T_1g, C3)  = 0            [120 deg, exact null]
  chi(T_1g, C2)  = -1           [180 deg]

The sign matters: positive chi means the T_1g mode is constructively activated
(pressure focusing, charge directed inward/upward); negative chi means
destructive coupling (charge scattered); zero means no coupling at all.
[AC1-AC7, 14/14 PASS]


SECTION 3: PIEZOELECTRICITY AS MAXWELL-CRITICAL TUNING
--------------------------------------------------------

3.1 MECHANISM

Crystalline quartz (SiO4 tetrahedra in a helical arrangement) has approximate
icosahedral local symmetry at each Si-O4 tetrahedral cluster. In the unstressed
state, the local geometry sits slightly below Maxwell criticality: T_1g coupling
is weak and no spontaneous electric field appears. When mechanical stress is
applied along the crystallographic c-axis, the SiO4 clusters deform toward the
Maxwell-critical icosahedral configuration. As they approach criticality, T_1g
coupling activates and an electric field develops: the piezoelectric effect.

The generated voltage is proportional to:
  V ∝ (proximity to Maxwell criticality) × chi(T_1g, C_N)

where C_N is the rotation symmetry of the stress-deformed local geometry. For
stress along the quartz c-axis (which has approximate 3-fold symmetry):
  chi(T_1g, C3) = 0  → no piezo response along c-axis

For stress along the a-axis (2-fold symmetry):
  chi(T_1g, C2) = -1 → piezo response, with polarity depending on sign of
  the deformation toward vs away from Maxwell criticality.

3.2 PREDICTIONS

  (a) Optimal piezo material: crystal whose local geometry sits closest to
      Maxwell criticality at rest, requiring the least stress for activation.
      Prediction: quasicrystals (Al-Mn, Icosahedrite) are AT exact Maxwell
      criticality throughout. They are not piezoelectric by the standard
      argument (lack of polar axis), but they should exhibit anomalous T_1g
      coupling at specific resonance frequencies -- measurable as anomalous
      EM response under vibration, distinct from conventional piezoelectricity.

  (b) Optimal stress axis: the axis whose deformation has the HIGHEST
      |chi(T_1g, C_N)|. For quartz this predicts the a-axis > c-axis, which
      is consistent with measured quartz piezo coefficients (d11 > d33).

  (c) Chiral icosahedral cluster: a material with a permanently chiral
      icosahedral local structure would have permanent T_1g coupling with no
      applied stress -- a pyroelectric-like effect with no temperature
      dependence. No such material is currently known; this is a design target.

  (d) Curie temperature behavior: thermal fluctuations randomize the local
      geometry, degrading the Maxwell-critical configuration. The piezo
      coefficient should decrease with temperature as the local order
      degrades, vanishing at the Curie temperature when thermal disorder
      exceeds the Maxwell-criticality well depth.

3.3 RETRODICTIONS

The standard quartz piezo coefficient d11 ~ 2 pC/N is consistent with a
near-but-not-at-Maxwell-critical local geometry where a small finite stress
is needed to reach criticality. The framework does not currently derive the
numerical value of d11 from first principles (the gap from current geometry
to Maxwell criticality in unstressed quartz is not yet parameterised);
this is an open quantitative item.


SECTION 4: THE ELECTRON ESCALATOR — PENTAGONAL PYRAMID GEOMETRY
-----------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 MECHANISM

The optimal charge-concentrating pyramid has face slope arctan(1/phi) = 31.72
deg. This slope places each of the five triangular faces at exactly the
icosahedral face orientation — the same orientation as the top-cap faces of
a Jobson cell viewed along its C5 axis. [AC12, PASS]

Every electron arriving at any point on a given face hits Jobson cells at
this icosahedral orientation. With five faces all at the same orientation
(rotated 72° from each other around the apex axis), ALL face-area electrons
contribute coherently to the same compression pattern in the cells beneath
each face. The same mechanism operates along the edges: electrons from all
five edges strike the same cell sets along each edge. Both faces and edges
drive the same directed compression.

The compressed cells block lateral exit paths. When a new electron enters
the region it cannot push laterally — the compressed cells already occupy
those directions. The only remaining path is toward the apex, exactly as an
electron follows a proton through the medium. The geometry forces
directionality; charge accumulates at the apex as a consequence, not as a
cause.

Because the face-area contribution dominates (face area >> edge line area),
the critical manufacturing tolerance is face angle precision — hold
arctan(1/phi) = 31.72° to ±0.5° across the face. Edge straightness is a
secondary concern.

4.2 N-SIDED CHI TABLE AND PREDICTED CHARGE RATIOS

The T_1g coupling for an N-sided pyramid depends on the rotation angle
between opposite faces at the apex:

  3-sided pyramid: chi(T_1g, C3) = 0      → no coupling, no escalator (null)
  4-sided pyramid: chi(T_1g, C4) = -1     → defocusing, charge scattered
  5-sided pyramid: chi(T_1g, C5) = +phi   → focusing, charge directed to apex

The sign flip between 4-sided (negative) and 5-sided (positive) is the
strongest prediction: a 4-sided pyramid at the same slope should produce
LESS apex charge than the uncharged baseline, not merely less than 5-sided.

Predicted apex charge ratio:
  5-sided / 4-sided = |chi(C5)| / |chi(C4)| = phi / 1 = phi = 1.618  [AC13]

4.3 SCALING PREDICTION

Because the mechanism is driven by face-area cell compression (not edge-line
compression), the escalator signal scales as face area, which scales as k²
for a linear scale factor k. Comparing a 5 cm base pyramid to a 10 cm base
pyramid (k=2): signal ratio = 4x, not 2x. This is a clean discriminator
between a face-area mechanism and an edge-length mechanism. [AC14]

4.4 EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL

Build three pyramids in identical conductive metal (aluminium or copper),
identical apex sharpness, identical base size, but N = 3, 4, and 5 sides:

  5-sided: face slope 31.72°, base pentagon
  4-sided: face slope 31.72° (same slope, square base)
  3-sided: face slope 31.72° (same slope, triangular base)

Using the same face slope across all three controls for the classical
lightning-rod effect (tip sharpness), which would predict equal charge at
all three apexes. A measured ratio of 5-sided/4-sided ≈ phi = 1.618 and
3-sided ≈ 0 would confirm the mechanism.

Equipment: electrometer (~$30-$50), charged plate to drive current, three
pyramids (~$10-$20 in sheet aluminium or brass). Total bench cost < $50.

For the scaling test: build a second set of pyramids at 2x linear size.
If the escalator effect is real, the 10 cm pyramid should produce 4x the
apex charge of the 5 cm pyramid at the same driving voltage, not 2x.

4.5 AMBIENT ELECTRON SOURCE AND COOLING PREDICTION

The escalator is the same mechanism as piezoelectricity (Section 3), not a
different one. In a piezo crystal, the cells are normally misaligned; applied
pressure deforms them toward the Maxwell-critical icosahedral angle, temporarily
activating the T_1g coupling and separating charge. The pentagonal pyramid
(see Section 3.2c) is the permanent version: its face slope arctan(1/phi) =
31.72 deg matches the icosahedral face orientation exactly at rest. The T_1g
coupling is always active; no stress is needed. The escalator runs continuously,
aggregating free electrons from the surrounding environment — not from the
pyramid's own conduction electrons.

Ambient free electrons come from natural ionization (cosmic rays + natural
radioactivity produce ~2×10^6 ion pairs per m^3 per second at sea level,
giving an equilibrium free electron density ~10^9/m^3). These are continuously
replenished. The T_1g gradient guides approaching electrons toward the apex;
the compressed cells block lateral exit, exactly as in Section 4.1.

  Mode 1 (isolated pyramid): accumulates to Coulomb saturation Q_sat ~ 10^-11 C
    in < 1 second. Apex stays charged; any leakage is immediately refilled.
    Passive, self-maintaining, no power input required.

  Mode 2 (base wire to ground): continuous current; ground is the electron
    source. Rate set by eta × chi. A stationary 5-sided pyramid near ground
    should produce a continuous DC current with no moving parts.

COOLING PREDICTION: each collected electron carries thermal kinetic energy
~(3/2)kT ≈ 0.038 eV from the ambient air. When guided up the T_1g pressure
gradient this becomes potential energy — the kinetic energy does not return
to the air. The surrounding air loses the thermal energy of every collected
electron: P_cooling = (3/2)kT/e × I ≈ 0.038 V × I. Negligible at pA
currents from ambient ionization; measurable if a stronger electron source
(plasma, UV ionization) drives higher collection rates.


SECTION 5: RETRODICTIONS — EXISTING EVIDENCE
---------------------------------------------

The following observations are consistent with T_1g coupling at icosahedral
geometry. None were predicted by torsionverse before being observed; they
are retrodictions that the mechanism naturally accounts for:

  Chladni patterns (standing wave nodes): sand grains accumulate at pressure
  nodes of standing acoustic waves. The T_1g minimum is the low-pressure
  point where the medium coupling is weakest. Small objects (lighter than
  the medium displacement threshold) accumulate here, distinct from larger
  objects driven by radiation pressure. This explains why Chladni patterns
  are mass-dependent: large grains follow radiation pressure (outward);
  small grains follow the T_1g minimum (inward to node). [doc_acoustics.txt]

  Acoustic levitation: objects levitate at pressure nodes of standing
  acoustic waves. In the torsionverse picture: the T_1g minimum at the
  node is a stable orbit for objects coupled to the medium pressure mode.
  Standard acoustic levitation theory (radiation pressure) gives the same
  qualitative result but does not predict N-dependence of levitation
  stability for arrays of sources.

  Ringing rocks: certain rocks ring metallically when struck, suggesting
  internal crystal resonance at icosahedral packing frequencies. The T_1g
  mode at these frequencies produces an internal pressure minimum that
  reduces the effective weight of the rock when driven at resonance.
  This is the acoustic analog of the spinning pyramid effect (Section 6
  of companion paper doc_series2_spinning_pyramid.txt).

  Newgrange chamber acoustics: the Newgrange passage tomb chamber has a
  computed spherical-cavity fundamental of ~57 Hz and a Helmholtz
  (passage+chamber) mode of ~1.2 Hz. Published archaeoacoustic measurements
  report the chamber resonance at ~110 Hz. The spherical cavity estimate
  is within a factor of 2; the discrepancy is consistent with the non-
  spherical actual chamber geometry. [AC8-AC9, PASS]

  Stonehenge inner horseshoe: the five trilithon pairs of the inner
  horseshoe form a C5-symmetric arrangement. This places a T_1g focus
  (chi = phi) at the altar stone position — the geometric and acoustically
  optimal location within the structure. This is a retrodiction of the
  altar stone placement, not a prediction.


SECTION 6: DISCUSSION
-----------------------

6.1 THE UNIFYING QUANTITY IS ALWAYS chi(T_1g)

Piezoelectricity, charge concentration, acoustic node patterns, and ringing
rock resonance are all governed by chi(T_1g, C_N) from the I_h character
table. The same group-theoretic number phi = 1.618 appears as:
  - The T_1g coupling maximum at C5 (72 deg)
  - The predicted 5-sided/4-sided charge ratio
  - The T_1g pressure-mode character that drives piezo activation
  - The phi² A_g enhancement for two-source acoustic interference [AC10]
  - The golden angle (137.5 deg = 360 - phi*180) in phyllotaxis
  - The N=10 ring coupling chi = phi² [AC5]

This convergence is not coincidental within the torsionverse framework:
phi is the character of the I_h T_1g mode at the C5 rotation, and C5
(72 deg, pentagonal) is the most efficient coupling angle in the
icosahedral medium. Any physical system with approximate 5-fold symmetry
will couple preferentially to T_1g at this angle.

6.2 CONNECTION TO SERIES 1

The T_1g mode is identified in Series 1 as the EM/pressure mode of the
icosahedral medium — the same mode responsible for electrostatics and
gravity at macroscopic scales (doc_jobson_cell.txt). The electron escalator
and piezoelectricity are human-scale manifestations of the same T_1g
pressure-following behavior that underlies all of electromagnetism in the
torsionverse. The medium does not distinguish between nuclear-scale and
bench-scale T_1g activation; the coupling law chi(T_1g, C_N) is the same
at all scales.

6.3 OPEN ITEMS

  - Formal derivation of piezo coefficient magnitude from medium shear
    modulus G_shear, density rho, and Rs (the proximity-to-Maxwell-
    criticality parameter is not yet quantified for specific crystals).
  - Quasicrystal anomalous EM response measurement: Al-Mn or Icosahedrite
    at exact Maxwell criticality predicted to show anomalous T_1g response
    under vibration; no measurement currently exists.
  - Optimal chiral icosahedral material: permanent T_1g coupling without
    applied stress. Design target for zero-stress voltage generation.


SECTION 7: CONCLUSIONS
------------------------

Two classes of T_1g coupling have been derived:

1. Piezoelectricity: stress drives a crystal toward Maxwell criticality
   (3V-E=6), activating T_1g. Voltage ∝ chi(T_1g, C_N). Optimal stress
   axis predicts a-axis > c-axis for quartz. Quasicrystals predicted to
   show anomalous EM response at icosahedral resonance frequencies.

2. Electron escalator: pentagonal pyramid at face slope arctan(1/phi) =
   31.72 deg presents the icosahedral face orientation on all five faces.
   Cell compression channels charge to apex. Predicted ratio:
   5-sided / 4-sided = phi = 1.618 [AC13]. Effect scales as face area
   k² [AC14], not edge length k. 4-sided defocuses; 3-sided is null.

Both predictions are falsifiable at bench scale for under $50. The
unifying parameter throughout is chi(T_1g, C5) = phi from the I_h
character table. [14/14 PASS, AC1-AC14]
