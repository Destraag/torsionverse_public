6.1  Bernoulli mechanism for precession: retrodiction

  A spinning gyroscope rim drags the torsion medium at the equator. By Bernoulli:
  lower pressure at the equatorial rim; higher pressure at the poles. When a
  torque tips the spin axis, part of the low-pressure equatorial belt moves into
  a higher-pressure region. The pressure-gradient force is a restoring torque
  perpendicular to both spin and applied torque -> precession, not further tipping.

  At lab scale: torsion medium density is negligible vs air drag. The mechanism
  is correct but the medium Bernoulli term is unobservable at lab scale; standard
  mechanics applies. This is a retrodiction: same result, new physical origin.

  At Zone 2 scale (molecular, nuclear, neutron star): medium pressure IS
  the dominant mechanism. NMR precession and pulsar spin-down are Zone 2 phenomena.

6.2  Two-axis fluid gyroscope: zero cross-coupling (exact)

  For a 2-axis fluid gyroscope (fluid circulating in orthogonal rings), the
T_1g modes along the two axes decouple exactly:

  chi(T_1g_x * T_1g_y, C_2) = (-1) * (-1) = +1 = chi(A_g)

  A_g is the totally-symmetric I_h representation: zero cross-coupling is exact,
not approximate. This is a new structural result: the decoupling follows from
group theory, not from approximate symmetry or engineering tolerance.

  PREDICTION: A fluid gyroscope built with exact C5 symmetry in its ring

6.3  Critical size R_crit and pulsar timing glitches

  The medium shear wave speed v_s = Rs * c limits how quickly the medium can

  R_crit = v_s * tau_relax ≈ 355 AU

rates become time-varying on the tau_relax timescale.

  PULSAR TIMING GLITCHES: pulsars are rotating neutron stars with Zone 2 as

  Falsifiable: compile glitch recovery times across known glitching pulsars;
