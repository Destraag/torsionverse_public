Zone 2 geometry is IDENTICAL for proton and neutron (N_J = 21, same Maxwell-
critical boundary, same I_h icosahedral surface). The 4 equatorial cog teeth
are present in the neutron but the Hopf winding is NOT engaged:

  1. The neutron Zone 1 quarks form a T_1g diquark (d-d pair antisymmetric)
  2. A_g(T_1g x T_2g) = 0: T_1g diquark cannot couple to Zone 2 T_2g field
  3. Without winding coupling: no Zone 3 frame-dragging -> no co-rotating
     Zone 3 pressure field -> no Coulomb (EM) field
  4. No Hopf winding -> no Zone 3 spinning -> EM-inert

However: the d quarks DO reach Zone 3 via orbital extension (not cog
engagement). On each orbital pass, d quarks extend to r_grind = 2*lambda_p,
which is inside Zone 3 (lambda_p < r_grind < r_p). They hit rolling Jobson
cells mechanically. This is NOT the same as the proton's Hopf-driven co-
rotation -- it is a mechanical impact that produces the Rs shear deflection.

Consequence: the neutron has NO EM field (no Hopf co-rotation) but DOES
have mechanical Zone 3 contact (d quarks reach r_grind). Gravity only
(excluded volume V_p, same as proton to within m_n - m_p correction).
