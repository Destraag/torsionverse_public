1. THE CORE FINDING (2026-09-07)
----------------------------------

No script anywhere in the repo builds a real multi-cell spatial
arrangement: multiple Jobson cells with distinct 3D positions/orientations
relative to each other, packed around a central void (Zone 1) or forming
a shell (Zone 2/3). Checked exhaustively across:

  - analysis/nuclear/cog_geometry.py       -- ONE reference icosahedron,
    12 vertices, only 4 equatorial ones used ("cog teeth"). No per-cell
    coordinates. No 21-cell arrangement.
  - analysis/gravity/ih_lattice_phonon.py  -- 3Nx3N dynamical matrix where
    N=12 is ONE cell's own vertices, not a count of cells. Confirmed by
    reading the actual matrix-construction code, not just the docstring.
  - analysis/medium/mg_j6_dynamical_matrix_irrep_check.py -- same single-
    cell 12-vertex construction, explicitly described in its own docstring
    as "the ACTUAL discrete lattice" in contrast to a continuum argument --
    even the framework's OWN most literal treatment never exceeds one cell.
  - analysis/quantum/hopping_amplitude.py  -- same single-cell 36x36 matrix.
  - analysis/nuclear/proton_structure.py, nuclear_geometry.py,
    nz_stability.py, analysis/higgs/higgs_subrep_nj.py -- N_J (21.17 for
    the proton, and every other particle's N_J) is used EXCLUSIVELY as a
    scalar density/ratio (Compton wavelength / L_J), a regime indicator
    (sub-cell / boundary / bulk), never as a count of positioned objects.
  - docs/series1/doc_nucleus.txt, doc_jobson_cell.txt -- "N_J = 21 Jobson
    cells" is stated as a count/regime ("Maxwell jamming boundary"), never
    as a claim about specific cell positions.

CONCLUSION: N_J=21 (and every other N_J value in the framework) is a
density/scaling number -- structurally the same KIND of quantity as "how
many atoms fit inside a Debye sphere" -- not a claim that 21 discrete,
individually-positioned objects exist and can be pointed to. Nothing in
the repo was ever built to answer "which specific cell is where" for any
zone of any particle.


2. THE TWO CATEGORIES OF CLAIM (why "everything" is NOT equally affected)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not every claim that mentions N_J, Zone 2, or cell contact needs real
multi-cell positions to be meaningful. Two structurally different kinds of
claim got tangled together across the repo's history, and they need to be
triaged separately:

  CATEGORY A -- STATISTICAL / SYMMETRY CLAIMS (provisionally OK):
    Use ONE reference cell's symmetry (character table, Descartes angular
    defect, spring-network eigenmodes) to make a claim about BULK/AVERAGED
    behavior. Which specific cell is physically where does not matter --
    only that the local symmetry is icosahedral. Analogous to deriving a
    crystal's bulk elastic-tensor symmetry from one unit cell without
    simulating every atom. Examples: Coulomb field isotropy (Descartes
    defect = 4*pi), elastic constants (nu, K/G) from the single-cell
    spring network, the n=18 gravity exponent, the atomic shell 2n^2 rule,
    Higgs=A_g. NOT obviously broken by the missing packing model, though
    NOT yet exhaustively re-verified that none of them secretly smuggle in
    a trajectory claim under statistical language (see Section 4, open).

  CATEGORY B -- TRAJECTORY CLAIMS (dead without real multi-cell geometry):
    Require tracking a specific object through a specific sequence of
    spatially-positioned contacts over time. Examples: cog_geometry.py's
    literal "string endpoint meshes with cog tooth N, then cog tooth M"
    narrative; the entire quark-flip/beta-decay mechanism (any version --
    weak_quark_flip.py [archived], weak_quark_edge_hop.py, or
    quark_flip_path_search.py); by extension, ANY doc text that describes
    a quark "hopping," "bouncing to," or "reading as" a specific OTHER
    vertex/tooth/cell.
    NOTE: cog_geometry.py's WINDING NUMBER CONCLUSION (=2, the (1,2) Hopf
    result) might still be rescuable as a pure symmetry/topological
    statement independent of the literal meshing narrative -- this is
    listed as OPEN in Section 4, not yet resolved either way.

  NEW CANDIDATE (author hypothesis, 2026-09-07, NOT YET INVESTIGATED):
    "Color charge" (SU(3) from icosahedral face 3-coloring, the 2G gluon
    modes, face_gluon_geometry.py) may not be a fundamental, intrinsic
    quantum number at all -- it may be a relabeling of the ORIENTATION of
    the surrounding Zone 2 cells relative to the confined quark (i.e.
    which specific cells/faces are geometrically available to interact,
    given the quark's actual position and the real 3D arrangement of
    Zone 2). If so, "color" is a RELATIONAL/geometric property (Category
    B, depends on real cell positions) currently dressed up in the
    LANGUAGE of an intrinsic charge (Category A, symmetry-only). This is
    structurally the SAME kind of category confusion this whole file is
    about -- untestable either way without the same missing multi-cell

    rewrite the color/gluon sections) given the proton/nucleus is not

teeth" pointing inward toward Zone 1 -- must have been established by SOME

Working hypothesis: this WAS computed at some point, inline (python -c or
equivalent, never saved to a script file), and the reasoning is now
permanently unrecoverable -- structurally identical to the CONFIRMED
concrete harm case already logged in
/memories/repo/torsionverse-policy.md ("20 faces converging to center /
W-Z directed cone" -- commit 2e946a7, no backing script ever existed).

zero exceptions going forward -- this is now the SECOND confirmed (or
